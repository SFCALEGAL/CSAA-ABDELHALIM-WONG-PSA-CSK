Subject: CGC-25-631802: Objection to the September 22 supplemental brief

Tyler and Chad,

The Court asked two questions. Whether Department 302 has jurisdiction to
hear the vexatious-litigant motion, and what to do with Pittman. Six pages.
That was the assignment.

The brief starts there. It does not stay there. By Section C it is deciding
probability of prevailing on a demurrer the Court never ruled. By Section D
it is six pending actions, an appeal that is not final, a Contra Costa
complaint, and an email from this morning. The conclusion then asks the
Court to grant the motion. The case is stayed. The hearing is October 6 so
the Court can decide whether it can hear the motion at all.

The declaration is the same paper with the page limit taken off. Eighty
pages, three exhibits, offered to prove the merits the order did not ask
you to brief. If the six pages had been enough for that, the exhibits would
not have been necessary.

I am serving Plaintiff's objection, the receipt declaration, the compliance
declaration, the proposed order, and the proof of service. The objection
asks the Court to strike the passages and the declaration that leave the
August 26 order, and to hear on October 6 only the two questions the Court
actually asked. Sections A and B, where they address those questions, are
left standing. You are welcome to defend them then.

Franciscus Dylan Rosario
 Plaintiff in pro per
